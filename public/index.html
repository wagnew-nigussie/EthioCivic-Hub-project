If a general ticket remains unaddressed beyond 48 hours (or an emergency beyond 4 hours), the database marks is_sla_breached=true and elevates the status to ESCALATED_UNRESOLVED. The ticket is automatically highlighted in Tab 4 (SLA Overdue Queue) and an alert is sent to municipal department heads.
