The backend triage engine evaluates flags across every channel: keyword analysis (e.g. "FLOOD", "TRAPPED", "ጎርፍ"), water depth telemetry (knee/waist deep), trapped persons count, and emergency shortcodes (*840*911# or the Flood SOS button).
